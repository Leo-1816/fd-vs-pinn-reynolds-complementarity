1D Reynolds, starved case with the void fraction
================================================

This is the full 1D problem of :ref:`sec-equations`. Only a thin oil layer
:math:`H_{\text{oil}}` arrives at the contact. The film becomes full at the
inlet meniscus :math:`X_a`, carries pressure up to :math:`X_b`, then breaks up
again. Upstream of :math:`X_a` and downstream of :math:`X_b`, the gap is only
partly filled with oil: there, :math:`P = 0` and the void fraction
:math:`\theta` is positive.

Unlike the previous chapters, nothing is imposed by hand: the two boundaries
:math:`X_a` and :math:`X_b` both come out of the solution.
